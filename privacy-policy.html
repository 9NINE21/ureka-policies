Our Business Messaging Service

Ureka Automation enables businesses to manage customer communications and automated replies through connected messaging accounts, including WhatsApp. This policy covers information relating both to business users of the Application and to people who communicate with those businesses through the Service.

When We handle customer conversations for a business client, We process those communications to provide the services requested by that client. The business client determines the purposes of its customer communications and is responsible for its own customer-facing privacy information. We also process information for Our own account administration, support, and operation of the Service as described in this policy.

WhatsApp Information and Automation

Depending on the features used and the information included in a conversation, We process sender and recipient phone numbers, message content and message types, message identifiers, timestamps, delivery or read-status events, and WhatsApp Business Account and phone-number identifiers. Message content may include personal information supplied by the sender. An incoming event may also contain attachment identifiers or metadata; the current fixed-reply workflow does not need to download attachment contents to reply.

We use this information to route messages to the appropriate business, provide predefined automated replies, and investigate delivery or technical issues. Our workflows may use n8n to process messages; workflow execution records can contain message content and related metadata. Meta/WhatsApp processes communications through its platform under its own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Current Testing and Planned AI Features

During the current testing phase, the WhatsApp integration sends predefined, fixed replies. This reply workflow does not send conversation content to an AI model to generate responses or use it to train a model. Messages and associated metadata are still processed by the messaging and automation systems, including workflow execution records as described above.

We plan to introduce AI-assisted replies. Before enabling model processing of personal information, We will update this policy to explain the AI providers or self-hosted models used, the information processed, processing locations, retention arrangements, and whether any information is used for model training. Any notices or permissions required for that processing will be addressed before the feature is enabled.
